60 """ 

61 Simulate noise from point, line and polygon sources on a regular grid of receivers. 

62 

63 An alternative to :func:`simulate_noise` built on the receiver-grid approach used by engineering 

64 noise models. Every receiver collects sound energy from every source along: 

65 

66 * the direct path, when no obstacle blocks it; 

67 * first-order reflections from obstacle walls (image-source method), when ``reflections`` is True; 

68 * diffraction around one or two corners of an obstacle (vertical building edges) into its shadow, when 

69 ``diffraction`` is True. 

70 

71 Each path loses ``20 lg r`` (geometric spreading, ``r`` in metres), air absorption ``k r`` (``k`` from 

72 temperature and frequency, as in :func:`simulate_noise`), a reflection loss ``-10 lg(1 - a)`` per 

73 reflection (``a`` = absorption ratio of the wall) and, through vegetation, the foliage rate of 

74 :func:`simulate_noise` times the length crossed. A diffracted path has the level at the direct distance 

75 minus the barrier attenuation of ISO 9613-2 without the ground term, ``10 lg(3 + 40 C3 δ / λ)``: ``δ`` is 

76 how much longer the path through the corners is, ``λ`` the wavelength, ``C3 = 1`` around one corner 

77 (Maekawa's formula) and ``(1 + (5 λ / e)²) / (1/3 + (5 λ / e)²)`` around two corners ``e`` metres apart. 

78 The attenuation is at most 20 dB around one corner and 25 dB around two. Paths and sources add up as 

79 energies, so two equal sources give +3 dB. Receivers inside obstacles are skipped. The levels are then 

80 grouped into bands of ``db_sim_step`` dB and returned as polygons, like :func:`simulate_noise`. 

81 

82 The computation runs in parallel in ObjectNat's Rust extension. 

83 

84 Args: 

85 source_points: 

86 Noise sources. Points are point sources. Lines (roads, railways) are line sources, sampled 

87 every ``source_spacing`` metres. Polygons (industrial sites, noisy buildings) radiate from 

88 their outline as line sources. Optional columns ``source_noise_db`` (level at 1 m from the 

89 point or line, dB) and ``geometric_mean_freq_hz`` override the arguments of the same name 

90 per source. 

91 obstacles:

101 trees: 

102 Vegetation polygons; sound crossing them is attenuated. 

103 air_temperature: 

104 Air temperature, °C. 

105 target_noise_db: 

106 Lowest level to report, dB. Sources reach as far as their own level drops to it. 

107 db_sim_step: 

108 Width of the output level bands, dB. 

109 reflections: 

110 Include first-order reflections from obstacle walls. 

111 reflection_max_dist: 

112 Only walls closer than this to a source reflect it, metres (default 50, as the maximum 

113 reflection distance of engineering models). Larger values are slower. 

114 diffraction: 

115 Bend sound around the corners of obstacles: a receiver hidden from a source but seen from a 

116 corner that the source sees at the edge of an obstacle gets the diffracted sound, and along the 

117 shadowed side of the obstacle's convex hull the sound also bends around the next corner. Corners 

118 are the convex vertices of the merged outlines simplified by 0.5 m, and the ends of lines.

343def _vertex_corners(xy: np.ndarray, *, closed: bool, two_sided: bool) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]: 

344 """Corner rows (see :func:`_diffraction_corners`) of the vertices of one ring or line, and which vertices 

345 they are.""" 

346 if len(xy) < 2: 346 ↛ 347line 346 didn't jump to line 347 because the condition on line 346 was never true

347 return np.empty((0, 8)), np.zeros(len(xy), dtype=bool) 

348 if closed: 348 ↛ 351line 348 didn't jump to line 351 because the condition on line 348 was always true

349 prev, nxt = np.roll(xy, 1, axis=0), np.roll(xy, -1, axis=0) 

350 else: # the ends of a line have one neighbour, given as both 

351 prev, nxt = np.vstack([xy[1:2], xy[:-1]]), np.vstack([xy[1:], xy[-2:-1]]) 

352 to_prev, to_next = prev - xy, nxt - xy 

353 length_prev, length_next = np.hypot(*to_prev.T), np.hypot(*to_next.T) 

354 ok = (length_prev > 1e-9) & (length_next > 1e-9) 

355 u_prev = to_prev / np.where(ok, length_prev, 1.0)[:, None] 

356 u_next = to_next / np.where(ok, length_next, 1.0)[:, None] 

357 sine = u_prev[:, 1] * u_next[:, 0] - u_prev[:, 0] * u_next[:, 1] # > 0: a left turn 

358 convex = np.abs(sine) > MIN_CORNER_SINE if two_sided else sine > MIN_CORNER_SINE 

359 keep = ok & (convex | np.all(prev == nxt, axis=1)) 

360 away = -(u_prev + u_next)[keep] # from the corner away from the obstacle 

361 outside = xy[keep] + CORNER_OFFSET * away / np.hypot(*away.T)[:, None] 

362 return np.hstack([xy[keep], prev[keep], nxt[keep], outside]), keep
